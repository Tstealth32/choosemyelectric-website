Compare Electric Rates by ZIP Code

Choose My Electric lets shoppers in Pennsylvania, Ohio, and Texas start with a simple ZIP code search. Use the web to compare electric rates and suppliers quickly, then move into the app for alerts, tracking, bill uploads, and future switching reminders.

See local electricity plans without a long setup

A ZIP code search is the fastest way to see whether your area has competitive electricity suppliers, what current rates look like, and whether there may be a lower plan than the one you have today. It gives you a light comparison experience that is useful for research, especially if you are still early in the process and just want to understand the market.

The web estimate is intentionally limited so it stays fast and simple. You can compare live supplier options, but the app is where you get the deeper tools that help you stay ahead over time.

When to use the website and when to use the app

The website is best for a first pass. If you want to compare electric suppliers, see what rates are available, and understand whether it is worth switching, the web tool does that well.

The app becomes more valuable when you want to upload your bill, keep track of your current plan, monitor a contract end date, or get a reminder before a promotional rate expires. That is the difference between a one-time search and a repeatable system for not overpaying.

  • Use the website to compare electric rates by ZIP code.
  • Use the app to track your plan and get rate expiration alerts.
  • Use the app when you want personalized rate matching from your bill.
