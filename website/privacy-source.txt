      <li>information extracted from your bill, such as utility name, supplier name, account-related details, usage, rates, billing dates, service address, and related bill fields</li>
      <li>manually entered bill details</li>
      <li>enrollment or supplier sign-up details, such as your name, email address, phone number, mailing or billing address, service address, ZIP code, and account number</li>
      <li>support messages you send to us</li>
    </ul>

    <p>When you use the App, we may collect:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>anonymous app install identifier</li>
      <li>app version and basic device/app activity data</li>
      <li>feature usage events, such as app opens, bill saves, rate searches, comparison views, sign-up clicks, completed supplier selections, and game launches</li>
      <li>notification/reminder preferences</li>
      <li>coarse location context you provide through the App, such as service state, city, and ZIP code</li>
    </ul>

    <p>If you buy AI scan credits or similar in-app products, purchases are processed by Apple or Google, as applicable. We may receive transaction metadata needed to confirm the purchase, restore credits, prevent fraud, and provide support. We do not receive your full payment card number.</p>

    <p>We use information we collect to:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>upload, scan, and analyze utility bills</li>
      <li>extract bill fields and help populate bill review screens</li>
      <li>compare utility and supplier offers</li>
      <li>show rates, estimated savings, and supplier recommendations</li>
      <li>save your current plan and supplier history</li>
      <li>help you complete supplier enrollment flows</li>
      <li>prefill enrollment or supplier application information when available</li>
      <li>send contract expiration reminders and related notifications if you opt in</li>
      <li>support AI bill scanning and local/on-device fallback scanning</li>
      <li>improve app performance, reliability, and user experience</li>
      <li>measure product usage and understand adoption trends</li>
      <li>troubleshoot issues and respond to support requests</li>
      <li>comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>3. Bill Scanning, AI Processing, and Local Processing</h2>
    <p>The App supports bill scanning and document analysis.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>When you upload a bill image or PDF, the App may process the document locally on your device and/or send relevant content to our backend services for parsing and extraction.</li>
      <li>When AI-powered bill scanning is enabled, bill content may be sent through our backend to third-party AI service providers that help us extract structured bill information.</li>
      <li>In some situations, such as low connectivity, unavailable AI services, or credit limitations, the App may attempt a local fallback scan on the device instead.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>We use uploaded documents and extracted bill data to provide the bill review, rate comparison, and supplier recommendation features of the App.</p>

    <p>We use analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve it over time.</p>
    <p>Our analytics may include:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>anonymous install ID</li>
      <li>event name and timestamp</li>
      <li>app version</li>
      <li>optional state, city, and ZIP code associated with an event or uploaded service location</li>
    </ul>

    <p>Our backend is designed to store aggregated service-location analytics and event analytics for reporting and product planning. We do not use analytics to sell your personal information.</p>

    </ul>
