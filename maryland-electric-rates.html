Electric Rates in Maryland

Start a Maryland electricity comparison with the delivery utility on your bill and the offers currently available for your account. Supply choices and eligibility vary; a ZIP code or statewide supplier list alone cannot confirm that you can enroll.

Check your utility and current offers

Use the Maryland Public Service Commission’s MD Electric Choice marketplace to check supply options for your account. Match the utility on your bill, such as BGE, Pepco, Delmarva Power or Potomac Edison. If your account is with a cooperative or municipal system, confirm its participation and available offers directly instead of assuming retail choice.

For Baltimore households with BGE service, the Baltimore guide provides the local starting point. Pepco customers should also confirm whether the service address is in Maryland or the District of Columbia, which has a separate market.

Why Maryland may show fewer supplier offers

The PSC’s explanation of retail-market changes says some suppliers stopped offering residential supply after Maryland’s reforms. It describes price caps, contract-length limits and changes to billing. An empty offer list does not mean you must choose a plan in another utility territory.

Check how an offer will be billed, including whether you will receive separate supplier and utility bills. Compare the total charges for the same period. Confirm current details with the supplier and utility before enrolling.

Use your actual kWh and supply charges

Write down your current supply price, usage, supplier fees and contract end date. Compare eligible offers with your current contract and the utility’s Standard Offer Service where applicable. A price cap is not a promise that an offer will beat your current bill.

Example: 700 kWh at an illustrative 12¢/kWh is $84 for supply before fees, delivery and taxes. A difference of 1¢/kWh changes that supply cost by $7 at the same usage. Check each offer’s terms rather than treating a statewide average as an available plan.

Check energy-assistance eligibility before enrolling

If your household receives or recently received Office of Home Energy Programs assistance, review the PSC’s guidance for energy-assistance households. Specific approval and pricing protections apply to eligible retail supply offers. Verify that an offer qualifies for your household with the supplier or PSC.

Maryland electric choice questions

Does changing suppliers change outage service?

Your local utility continues to deliver electricity. Contact it for outages and service emergencies.

What if I cannot find a suitable offer?

Keep your current supply arrangement while checking its terms and your utility’s options. You do not have to choose an unsuitable offer because a comparison returns few results.

Where can I verify a supplier?

Use the official MD Electric Choice marketplace and its licensed-supplier information. Confirm the exact offer, account eligibility and final contract directly before enrolling.
