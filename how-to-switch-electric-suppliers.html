How to Switch Electric Suppliers

Compare your options, switch with better timing, and use the app to remember when it is time to compare again.

Step 1: Compare current options in your area

Start with your ZIP code. That gives you a market view of available electric suppliers and helps you see whether there are lower rates than the one you have now. You do not need a long setup just to understand whether switching is worth considering.

Step 2: Review the plan details

Look beyond the first number you see. Think about how long the rate lasts, whether it is fixed or not, and whether you may need to compare again soon. If you can upload a bill in the app, you may also get a better sense of whether a plan fits your usage and current situation.

Step 3: Enroll with the new provider

Once you choose a supplier, the enrollment step usually happens through the supplier. Choose My Electric is there to help you compare, decide, and stay organized, but the final switch is handled through the provider you select.

After that, the most important move is to remember when to compare again. That is why the app pushes users toward alerts and plan tracking rather than making the web version overly complicated.
