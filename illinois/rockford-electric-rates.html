Rockford, Illinois

Rockford Electric Rates

Rockford households in ComEd territory should check both their current supply arrangement and the city’s municipal electrical aggregation information before comparing plans. ComEd continues to deliver electricity, while the supply portion can come from utility supply, an aggregation program, or an individual retail supplier.

Read the current Rockford aggregation notice

The City of Rockford publishes municipal electrical aggregation information, customer letters, and program documents. Use the notice for your account to check the supplier, eligibility, effective dates, and instructions for opting out. Program terms can change when a contract is renewed.

Compare those terms with the supplier name and price on your ComEd bill. Do not assume that every home with a Rockford postal address is enrolled in the same program. If the notice and your bill differ, confirm your account’s status before signing a separate supply contract.

Compare supply with ComEd’s benchmark

The Illinois Commerce Commission’s ComEd Price to Compare page provides the utility supply benchmark and its effective period. It is a reference for the supply portion of a bill, not a quote for the full cost of electricity delivered to your home.

Keep three figures available where applicable: your actual contracted supply price, the current aggregation offer, and ComEd’s benchmark. Use the same monthly kWh for each calculation. Include recurring supplier charges and read the disclosures about any adjustments instead of comparing only the largest advertised number.

Plan for seasonal usage and renewal

Look at several months of your household’s electricity use before choosing a plan. Cooling, electric heating, or vehicle charging can change the amount of supply you buy. A fixed price per kWh does not make the entire bill fixed, and a monthly supplier fee weighs more heavily on a low-usage month.

Save the contract’s end date, renewal language, cancellation terms, and supplier contact information. Check the next offer before the initial term expires. If renewable content matters to you, read how the supplier documents it rather than assuming that changing suppliers changes the electricity delivered on ComEd’s wires.

Rockford electric rate FAQ

Does Rockford have municipal electrical aggregation?

The city publishes an electrical aggregation program page and customer notices. Consult the current documents for your account’s eligibility, supplier, dates, and opt-out process.

Does joining an aggregation program change my delivery utility?

ComEd remains the delivery utility for a ComEd account. Aggregation concerns electricity supply; it does not replace the utility’s poles, wires, or outage service.

Is the lowest supply rate always the lowest total cost?

A rate alone may leave out recurring supplier fees or conditions. Compare all supply charges at the same usage, then account separately for utility delivery charges and other bill items.
