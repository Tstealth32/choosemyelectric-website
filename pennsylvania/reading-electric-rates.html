Reading, Pennsylvania

Reading Electric Rates

Reading households in Met-Ed territory can compare electricity suppliers while keeping their local delivery utility. Start with the Met-Ed rate district on your bill, your current supply price, and your monthly usage. A low advertised rate is only useful when the offer applies to your account and its fees fit your usage.

Find the Met-Ed supply benchmark on your bill

FirstEnergy’s Met-Ed bill guide shows where to find usage and charges. Keep the supply price separate from delivery charges when comparing a Reading electricity offer. If you already have a competitive supplier, record that supplier’s rate as well as the utility’s Price to Compare.

The Met-Ed customer-choice guide explains the local shopping process. Check the benchmark’s effective dates before estimating savings: a utility price that changes during your contract cannot support a guaranteed year of savings.

Choose the correct Reading utility territory

Enter your service ZIP code in the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission’s PA Power Switch comparison tool, then match the utility and customer class to your bill. Reading shoppers in Met-Ed territory should not substitute a PPL or PECO offer simply because it is advertised elsewhere in Pennsylvania.

If you are moving into a Reading apartment, establish who holds the electric account first. A renter with an individual account can review that account’s options; a building that includes electricity in rent may use a different billing arrangement. The name on the bill and the service address matter more than a city-wide advertised price.

Compare costs at your actual monthly usage

For each offer, multiply your expected kWh by the price in dollars per kWh and add recurring supplier fees. As an illustration, 600 kWh at 10¢ costs $60 for supply. Adding a $9 monthly supplier fee makes it $69. These are example numbers, not a Reading offer; utility delivery charges and taxes are additional.

Repeat the calculation for a higher-usage month if you use electric heating or air conditioning. Review the fixed or variable price, contract length, cancellation terms, and renewal notice together. Save the offer disclosure and a reminder to compare again before the initial term ends.

Reading electric rate FAQ

Who delivers electricity in Reading?

This guide covers Reading customers in the Met-Ed rate district. Confirm the delivery utility on your bill before selecting a supplier offer. Your delivery utility remains responsible for the local wires and outage service.

Is the Met-Ed Price to Compare my entire electric bill?

No. It is a supply benchmark. Delivery charges and other bill items still apply, and your existing competitive supplier may charge a different supply price.

Can I use an Allentown offer for a Reading home?

Only if its terms also cover your utility account. The Allentown guide focuses on PPL, while this Reading guide focuses on Met-Ed. Match the utility, customer class, and service ZIP before comparing.
