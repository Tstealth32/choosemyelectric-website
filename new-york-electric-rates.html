How to Compare Electric Rates in New York

As of July 15, 2026, New York is temporarily different from many other supplier-choice states because the official public Power to Choose application page is showing an offline notice. That means the clearest public comparison path right now is the utility benchmark plus the New York DPS supplier directory, not a normal plan-by-plan statewide rate board.

How electric choice works in New York

New York customers still compare supplier pricing against a utility context, but right now the public market view is benchmark-heavy because the usual Power to Choose application is unavailable. That makes the utility or load-zone selection on the bill especially important.

What shoppers can still use while the application is offline

The official utility benchmark remains useful, and the New York DPS supplier directory still shows which ESCOs are active. The DPS help page also still explains how the service normally works. Together they support a real research flow even without the usual public plan cards.

Official sources: Power to Choose application page, Power to Choose help page, and How to shop for energy services.

New York electric rate FAQ

Why are there no normal live plan cards for New York right now?

Because the official public Power to Choose application page is offline as of July 15, 2026, so the safest public fallback is the utility benchmark plus the NY DPS supplier directory.

Does that mean New York supplier choice is gone?

No. The supplier-choice market still exists. The public offer-board source is what is temporarily offline.

What should shoppers do while the board is down?

Use the utility benchmark, identify the service area or load zone on the bill, review the active supplier directory, and keep the app ready for tracking and follow-up.
