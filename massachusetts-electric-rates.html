How electric choice works in Massachusetts

Massachusetts shoppers usually compare supplier offers inside utility territories such as Eversource, National Grid, or Unitil. Municipal light plant customers often face a different structure, which is why statewide supplier messaging can sound broader than the actual choice map on the ground.
