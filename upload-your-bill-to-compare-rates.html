Upload Your Bill to Compare Electric Rates

A bill upload gives Choose My Electric more context than a ZIP code alone. It helps match your current supplier, rate, and usage information so the comparison can be more personalized.

Compare rates on the web. Track your plan in the app before your rate jumps.

Why a bill upload improves comparison

A simple ZIP code search is a strong first step, but it cannot always tell the full story of what you are paying right now. A real bill often contains your current supplier, rate details, utility context, and usage information. That gives the app a better starting point for matching you to other plans.

What the website does and what the app adds

The website is intentionally limited to light comparison so it stays fast, simple, and search-friendly. Bill upload is an app feature. That separation makes the website useful for discovery while keeping the more advanced workflow inside the iPhone experience.

  • Website: ZIP code search and basic rate comparison.
  • App: Bill upload, plan tracking, and rate expiration alerts.

Use both together

Many shoppers start on the website to understand the market, then download the app once they want a more precise recommendation. That path works well because it keeps the research step easy while still giving you a stronger tool when you are serious about switching.
