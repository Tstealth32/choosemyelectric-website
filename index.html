FAQ

Short answers people will want before they install

What does Choose My Electric do?

It helps people check live market options by ZIP and, in the app, scan a bill, save plan details, and get alerts before their rate is about to expire.

What states are supported right now?

The current mobile flow is aimed at Pennsylvania, Texas, and Ohio residential users, including Ohio electric and gas use cases.

Will the app warn me before my rate expires?

Yes. That is one of the biggest reasons to use the app. It can help you stay ahead of contract or rate expiration so you have time to compare again before renewal.

Do customers need to type everything in manually?

No. On the website they can start with just a ZIP code. For bill-based autofill and a more personal estimate, they should use the app.

Can I start on the website and finish in the app?

Yes. The website is a great way to get a quick ZIP-based estimate, and the app is there when you want bill scanning, saved details, and renewal alerts.
