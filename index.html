FAQ

Short answers people will want before they install

What does Choose My Electric do?

It helps people check live market options by ZIP and, in the app, scan a bill to compare those options against their current setup.

What states are supported right now?

The current mobile flow is aimed at Pennsylvania, Texas, and Ohio residential users, including Ohio electric and gas use cases.

Do customers need to type everything in manually?

No. On the website they can start with just a ZIP code. For bill-based autofill and a more personal estimate, they should use the app.

Can people use it on the website too?

Yes. The website gives a ZIP-based estimate and live market look. The app is where bill scanning and saved bill history live.

What if the scan gets something wrong?

In the app, you can review the extracted information and fix anything that looks off before moving forward.
