Toledo, Ohio

Toledo Electric Rates

Toledo households served by Toledo Edison can compare electricity supply offers while keeping their local delivery utility. Start with the Price to Compare on your bill, then check whether your current supply comes from default service, a city aggregation program, or a separate supplier contract.

Compare offers for Toledo Edison territory

The Public Utilities Commission of Ohio provides a dedicated Toledo Edison residential comparison chart. Use that territory when checking offers. Ohio Edison and The Illuminating Company have separate comparison charts, even though they also belong to FirstEnergy.

The Price to Compare is a reference for the generation supply portion of the bill. It is not your total delivered electricity cost. The official chart also notes that qualifying electric-heating customers may have a different benchmark during part of the year; check the message section of your own bill.

Check your Toledo aggregation notice

Toledo participates in the Northwest Ohio Aggregation Coalition. The city’s aggregation page explains the program and eligibility. Municipal aggregation groups eligible customers for supply purchasing, so you may already have a supply arrangement without having selected a supplier individually.

Read the dates on any advertised program rate and use your latest enrollment or renewal notice to confirm the current term. An older city notice is not a current quote. Compare your existing arrangement with any new offer before enrolling, including opt-out instructions and any applicable contract conditions.

Compare the full cost and set a renewal reminder

Enter your ZIP code to begin the web comparison, then confirm Toledo Edison and the service details from your bill. Ohio’s official switching guide recommends using your bill’s Price to Compare when evaluating offers.

Compare the rate type, contract length, monthly fee, introductory period, and any cancellation fee. As an example, a 1¢/kWh difference at 800 kWh equals $8 in monthly supply cost before fees. Decide using your actual usage, and note when the introductory or fixed term ends so you can review the next price.

Toledo electric rate FAQ

Which utility comparison chart should a Toledo Edison customer use?

Select Toledo Edison on the Ohio Apples to Apples electric comparison site. Confirm the utility and customer class shown on your bill before comparing offers.

Is a city aggregation rate always my current rate?

No. Check the supplier and price on your current bill and the dates in your latest aggregation notice. Eligibility, enrollment, and contract periods matter.

Will my Toledo Edison delivery charges disappear if I switch?

No. Supplier shopping affects electricity supply. Your delivery utility and its delivery charges remain part of the bill.
