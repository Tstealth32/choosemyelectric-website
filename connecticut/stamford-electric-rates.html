Stamford, Connecticut

Stamford Electric Rates

Stamford households with an Eversource electric account can compare the supply portion of their bill with Eversource Standard Service and eligible third-party offers. Start by confirming who supplies your electricity now. A different supplier price changes only part of what you pay each month.

Start with Eversource Residential

Eversource’s Connecticut supplier-options guide directs residential customers to the Energize CT Supplier Rate Finder. Select Eversource Residential so you compare offers against the appropriate Standard Service rate.

Record the supply rate and supplier name on your current bill before comparing. Standard Service is the default supply option, but you may already have a third-party contract. Compare a new offer with both your actual price and the default rate, using the effective dates for each.

Separate supply from delivery

Connecticut’s Office of Consumer Counsel explains the distinction in its electricity supplier guide. Eversource continues to deliver electricity for its customers regardless of which eligible company supplies it. Switching supply does not remove delivery charges or change your outage contact.

For a Stamford apartment or condominium, check whether you hold an individual Eversource account or pay for electricity through the building. Review the service address and account class before selecting an offer. A neighboring household’s plan is not a substitute for checking your own account.

Compare the term as well as the rate

Eversource’s residential shopping guidance states that Connecticut supplier rates must be fixed, last at least four months, and cannot include early termination or cancellation fees. Read the current disclosure and renewal notice so you know the exact price period and what happens afterward.

Estimate supply cost by multiplying the price in dollars per kWh by your expected usage, and review the offer for any other charges. Compare several months if your home uses electric heating, cooling, or vehicle charging. A fixed supply rate does not freeze usage or utility delivery charges, and today’s Standard Service comparison may change later.

Stamford electric rate FAQ

Which utility should I select when comparing Stamford offers?

For a Stamford bill from Eversource, choose Eversource Residential in the Connecticut rate finder. Match the utility and account class shown on your bill.

Can my total bill change under a fixed supply contract?

Yes. A fixed supply price applies to each kWh during its contract period. Usage, delivery charges, and other bill items can still change the amount due.

How do I compare an offer with Standard Service?

Use the current Eversource Standard Service supply rate and its effective dates. Compare the same kWh usage and the full offer terms, then revisit the calculation when either price period ends.
