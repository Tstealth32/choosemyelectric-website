Privacy

Choose My Electric Privacy Summary

Last updated: April 8, 2026

What we collect

Depending on how you use Choose My Electric, we may collect ZIP code and service location details, utility and supplier selections, bill photos or PDFs, bill fields extracted from uploaded documents, manually entered bill details, enrollment details, support messages, app usage events, purchase metadata, and notification preferences.

How we use it

We use this information to analyze bills, populate review screens, compare supplier offers, show rates and estimated savings, save bill history, support reminder flows, improve product performance, and respond to support or legal obligations.

Bill scanning and AI processing

Uploaded bills may be processed locally on the device and/or sent through backend services for parsing and structured extraction. When AI-powered bill scanning is enabled, bill content may be processed by third-party AI service providers used to support extraction.

How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help operate the apps and backend, process bill scans, deliver notifications, support in-app purchases, or comply with legal and security obligations. We do not sell personal information for money.

Your choices

You can decide whether to upload bills, manually enter information, allow notifications, or contact us with privacy and deletion requests. You can also uninstall the apps to stop future collection from that device.

Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or support, contact contact@choosemyelectric.com.
