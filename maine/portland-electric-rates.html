Portland, Maine

Portland, Maine Electric Rates

Portland, Maine households receive electric delivery service from Central Maine Power (CMP). Your supply can come from the standard offer or a competitive electricity provider. Start by checking which supply service appears on your CMP bill, then compare offers for the same utility district.

How CMP delivery and electricity supply fit together

CMP lists Portland in its service territory. CMP delivers your electricity; choosing a different supplier changes the supply arrangement while CMP continues to handle delivery.

For customers who do not choose a competitive provider, the Maine Public Utilities Commission arranges standard-offer supply. Use the Commission’s standard-offer page and select Central Maine Power and the appropriate customer class. A Versant district benchmark is not the right starting point for a Portland CMP account.

Compare the supply rate with your own usage

Keep a recent CMP bill nearby. Note your supplier name, supply price per kilowatt-hour, monthly usage, and any contract end date. Compare a competing offer against the CMP standard offer for the period you expect to use it. A statewide average that includes delivery is a different measure.

As an illustration, a 1¢/kWh supply difference at 600 kWh is $6 for that month before any added supplier fees. It is not a $6 reduction in delivery charges or a promise of savings. Use your own usage and the full contract terms when deciding whether a switch is worthwhile.

Read the Maine supplier terms before enrolling

The Maine Commission’s competitive-provider guidance is a starting point for checking suppliers. Confirm the offer applies to your CMP account and ask how long its price lasts, whether it can vary, and what happens when the term ends.

Choose My Electric’s Maine guide explains the limits of public supplier samples. A public listing may not represent every available offer. Use the official sources to confirm current terms, and use the app for bill-based comparison and plan tracking.

Portland electric rate FAQ

Is this guide for Portland, Maine or Portland, Oregon?

This page covers Portland, Maine and Central Maine Power service. Utility rules and supply choices in Portland, Oregon are different.

Do I have to choose a competitive supplier?

No. Maine customers who do not choose a competitive electricity provider receive standard-offer supply. Compare any alternative with the CMP standard offer before deciding.

Does switching suppliers remove CMP delivery charges?

No. Electricity supply and delivery are separate parts of the bill. Choosing a supplier does not remove CMP delivery charges.
