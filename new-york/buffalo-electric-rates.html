Buffalo Electric Rates

Buffalo gives Western New York shoppers a clearer local entry point while the public Power to Choose offer board is still offline. Many homes in the Buffalo area are in National Grid territory, which makes utility context especially important before you compare supplier options more deeply.

Buffalo shoppers still have supplier choice, but the public comparison flow looks different right now because the official offer board remains down. The safest public starting point is still benchmark-and-directory first: identify the utility territory, understand the benchmark context, and review which suppliers remain active.

National Grid still delivers the electricity, handles outages, and manages the local delivery relationship. Supplier choice affects the supply side of the bill, not the physical grid service. That distinction helps Buffalo users compare more calmly and keep the research grounded in the right utility context.

Buffalo is also a useful city page because it broadens New York coverage beyond the Con Edison focus of New York City. Western New York shoppers often want a local explanation first, especially while the normal public plan board is unavailable.
