Where live web comparison is strongest today

Enter a supported deregulated-state ZIP code to load the correct market flow. Pennsylvania, Texas, and Ohio can show live supplier comparisons quickly. Maryland, Massachusetts, Maine, New Jersey, Rhode Island, Illinois, and New York may also ask for the utility on the bill first, while Connecticut, DC, and New York can use official benchmark or directory data when a live statewide rate board is limited. The Ohio Electric or Gas option appears only after a full Ohio ZIP code is entered. Some non-supplier states may still hand off to official community solar paths instead of simply stopping at a dead end.
